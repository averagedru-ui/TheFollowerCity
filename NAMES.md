# Name ideas

Vibe: cozy, isometric, seasonal, community-built (every follower gets a home), a little bit Colorado.

## Top picks
| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| **Aspenglow** | Colorado aspens turning gold + that warm glow of a cozy town at dusk. Matches the fall palette. |
| **Pebblebrook** | Small, soft, storybook. Easy to say, easy to logo. |
| **Springhaven** | Nods to the folder name (TheFollowSprings) and "haven" = a place you belong. |
| **Followvale** | Direct play on "followers" + the valley/vale naming of the reference game, but ours. |
| **Hearthvale** | Hearth = cozy, home, warm lights in windows. |

## Cozy / storybook
Thistledown, Willowmere, Hollowbrook, Little Foxglove, Tumbleweed Row, Cobblestone & Pine, Nestwick, Mossgate, Honeybrook, Larkspur Lane

## Colorado flavored
Front Range, Mile High Mews, Foothill Folk, Aspen Ridge, Switchback, Cottonwood Crossing, Platte & Pine, Sunrise Summit

## Followers / community
The Follow Springs, Followfield, Folkvale, Neighborhood Watch (joke), Tinytown Tales, Pocket Town, Fellowship Falls

## Short + brandable
Sprig, Vale, Nestle, Burrow, Hamlet, Glen

## Before you commit
- Search the name + "game" on Steam, itch.io, App Store.
- Check the .com, .gg, .io and the handle on the platforms you stream on.
- Say it out loud in a sentence: "I'm building ____".
